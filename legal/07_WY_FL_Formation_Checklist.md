# Wyoming and Florida Formation Checklist

## Wyoming HoldCo
- Confirm name availability for Busy Bee Holdings LLC.
- Appoint and retain a Wyoming registered agent.
- File Articles of Organization.
- Obtain EIN from the IRS.
- Execute Operating Agreement.
- Open business bank account.

## Florida OpCo
- Confirm whether to form a Florida LLC or register a foreign LLC.
- If forming a new Florida LLC, file Articles of Organization.
- If registering a foreign LLC, prepare the authorization filing and certificate of existence.
- Obtain EIN if separate tax entity treatment is desired.
- Execute HoldCo to OpCo IP license.
