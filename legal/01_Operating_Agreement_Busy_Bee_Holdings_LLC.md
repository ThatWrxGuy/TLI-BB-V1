# OPERATING AGREEMENT OF BUSY BEE HOLDINGS LLC

This Operating Agreement is entered into effective as of __________, 2026, by and among the Members of Busy Bee Holdings LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company (the "Company").

## 1. Formation
The Company has been formed under the Wyoming Limited Liability Company Act. The Company name is Busy Bee Holdings LLC. The principal business purpose is to own, protect, license, and commercialize the Busy Bee platform and related intellectual property.

## 2. Members and Ownership
The initial capitalization and class structure are as follows:

| Member | Class | Percentage |
|---|---:|---:|
| Busy Bee Legacy Trust | Class B Control Units | 51% |
| Christopher Jordon Byrne | Class B Control Units | 29% |
| Investor Pool Reserve | Class A Economic Units | 20% |

No issuance may cause the Trust to fall below 51% without an amendment approved as a Reserved Action.

## 3. Management
The Company is manager-managed. Christopher Jordon Byrne shall serve as initial President and Chief Executive Officer. Additional officers may be appointed by the Board.

## 4. Board and Governance
Reserved Actions require all of the following:

1. Executive brief presented.
2. Approval of the Founder Representative.
3. Approval of the Trust Representative.
4. Board supermajority approval.

Reserved Actions include:
- sale, merger, conversion, or dissolution of the Company;
- issuance of new equity or rights to acquire equity;
- amendment of this Agreement or governance documents;
- transfer, assignment, exclusive license, pledge, or encumbrance of core Busy Bee IP;
- indebtedness above a threshold set by Board resolution;
- admission of new investors beyond the authorized Investor Pool;
- removal of the Founder from executive authority.

## 5. Transfers
No Member may transfer units except in compliance with this Agreement, the right of first refusal, and applicable law.

## 6. Intellectual Property
All Busy Bee IP shall be owned exclusively by the Company unless expressly designated in a signed written assignment or license consistent with this Agreement.

## 7. Books and Records
The Company shall maintain a cap table, governance log, executive brief archive, and audit trail for all Reserved Actions.
